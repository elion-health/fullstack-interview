PRODUCT FEEDBACK COMPILATION
=============================
Product: Epic MyChart Patient Portal
Health System: Massachusetts General Hospital
Compiled by: Patient Experience Team
Date: November 20, 2024
Feedback Period: Sept-Nov 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
MyChart usage continues to grow (now 73% of active patients enrolled). Overall satisfaction is 4.2/5, but several pain points emerged from focus groups and support tickets that need addressing. Mobile app usage has overtaken desktop (62% mobile vs 38% web).

POSITIVE FEEDBACK
-----------------

Theme: Appointment Self-Scheduling
- "Being able to book my own appointments is a game-changer. No more phone tag."
- "Love that I can see available times across different providers in the same specialty."
- "The reminder texts are perfect - haven't missed an appointment in over a year."

Theme: Test Results Access
- "Getting lab results with doctor's notes before my follow-up call helps me prepare questions."
- "The trend graphs for my A1C levels are really motivating. I can see my progress."
- "Appreciate the explanations of what the results mean - reduces my anxiety waiting for the doctor to call."

Theme: Prescription Management
- "Refill requests are so easy now. Used to have to call and wait on hold."
- "Love being able to see all my medications in one place."

CRITICAL ISSUES
---------------

1. Message Response Times (HIGH PRIORITY)
   Complaint Volume: 247 tickets

   - "I sent an urgent message about medication side effects and didn't hear back for 3 days."
   - "The portal says responses in 2 business days but I consistently wait 4-5 days."
   - "No way to mark a message as urgent - everything goes into the same queue."
   - "Would rather just call at this point - at least I get a response."

   Root Cause Analysis:
   - Clinical staff understaffed for message volume (up 40% YoY)
   - No triage system for message priority
   - Some departments better than others (Cardiology <24hrs, Ortho >4 days)

   Recommendation: Implement message triage AI + hire additional support staff

2. Mobile App Crashes (HIGH PRIORITY)
   Complaint Volume: 189 tickets (iOS users specifically)

   - "App crashes every time I try to upload a photo for my dermatology visit."
   - "Can't view my imaging results - app just freezes and I have to restart."
   - "After the last iOS update, the app crashes on launch 50% of the time."

   Technical Investigation:
   - Issue confirmed with iOS 18.1 and MyChart version 2024.3.1
   - Memory leak when loading large PDF documents
   - Image upload fails for files >8MB

   Status: Epic released patch 2024.3.2 on Nov 18, pushed to app store Nov 20
   Action: Monitor crash reports over next 2 weeks

3. Video Visit Quality (MEDIUM PRIORITY)
   Complaint Volume: 156 tickets

   - "Video froze multiple times during my appointment. We had to switch to phone."
   - "Audio echo made it hard to hear my doctor."
   - "Couldn't get video to work on my Android tablet, only works on my phone."

   Issue: MyChart video uses older WebRTC implementation, not optimized for low bandwidth
   Note: This is an Epic platform limitation, not something we can fix locally
   Workaround: IT team created troubleshooting guide, added bandwidth test to pre-visit checklist

4. Bill Pay Confusion (MEDIUM PRIORITY)
   Complaint Volume: 134 tickets

   - "I have 5 different bills showing up. Can't figure out what I actually owe."
   - "Paid my bill in MyChart but still receiving paper statements saying I owe money."
   - "The billing codes don't make sense - can't tell what service each charge is for."

   Root Cause: Integration issue between Epic and revenue cycle system
   - Payment sync delay of 3-5 days causing confusion
   - Multiple billing entities (hospital, physicians, labs) create separate bills

   Status: Revenue cycle team working on consolidation project (Q1 2025 target)

5. Proxy Access Issues (LOW-MEDIUM PRIORITY)
   Complaint Volume: 89 tickets

   - "I'm trying to manage my elderly mother's care but can't see her test results."
   - "Proxy access for my teenager expired and I can't figure out how to renew it."
   - "As a parent of twins, having to switch between two accounts is so tedious."

   Policy Issue: Proxy access requires in-person verification for security
   Friction: Parents of adult children (18+) must get signed consent forms

   Opportunity: Explore digital consent workflows

FEATURE REQUESTS
----------------

Top Requested Features (by volume):

1. Medication Price Comparison (78 requests)
   - "I want to see if there's a cheaper pharmacy for my prescriptions."
   - "Show me generic alternatives and what they cost at different pharmacies."

2. Direct Specialist Scheduling (64 requests)
   - "Why do I need a referral to book with a specialist in your own system?"
   - "Let me self-schedule with specialists like I can with my PCP."

3. Family Calendar View (52 requests)
   - "Managing appointments for 4 family members means switching accounts constantly."
   - "Need a combined calendar view of all my dependents' appointments."

4. Symptom Checker / Virtual Triage (49 requests)
   - "Would be great to input symptoms and get guidance on whether I need to come in."
   - "Help me decide if I need urgent care vs. regular appointment vs. ER."

5. Integration with Wearables (43 requests)
   - "Can my Apple Watch data feed into my chart so my doctor sees my heart rate trends?"
   - "Let me share my Fitbit sleep data with my sleep specialist."

COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE
-------------------------

Our team tested competitor patient portals:

Cleveland Clinic (Epic MyChart):
- Similar feature set to ours
- Better mobile app performance (fewer crash reports in app store reviews)
- Integrated wellness content and health coaching

UCSF (Cerner portal):
- Lower user satisfaction based on app store ratings (3.2/5 vs our 4.2/5)
- More flexible proxy access model
- Weaker prescription management features

Partners HealthCare (Epic MyChart):
- Recently launched AI chat assistant for appointment scheduling
- Allows patients to compare appointment wait times across locations
- Integrated telehealth triage before booking in-person

FOCUS GROUP INSIGHTS
--------------------

Conducted 3 focus groups with diverse patient populations (Nov 5-12, n=42 total)

Older Adults (65+, n=15):
- Prefer phone calls over portal messages for complex questions
- Struggle with password resets - need better support
- Want larger font options in mobile app
- Appreciate ability to add family members as proxy users

Working Adults (25-50, n=18):
- Value mobile-first experience and speed
- Want more self-service options to avoid phone calls during work hours
- Frustrated by fragmented billing experience
- Interested in AI features like symptom checking

Parents (n=9):
- Managing multiple kids' accounts is biggest pain point
- Love vaccination tracking features
- Want school forms and medical records export to be easier
- Interested in developmental milestone tracking for young children

2. Implement message triage/urgency classification

2. Improve video visit quality through network optimization
3. Consolidate billing statements (coordinate with revenue cycle)
4. Streamline proxy access renewal process

Medium-term (6-12 Months):
1. Evaluate Epic's medication price transparency module
2. Expand self-scheduling to more specialties
3. Pilot family calendar view feature
4. Integrate basic wearable data feeds (heart rate, steps, sleep)

Long-term (12+ Months):
1. Implement symptom checker / virtual triage tool
2. Explore AI chat assistant for common queries
3. Build personalized health content recommendation engine
